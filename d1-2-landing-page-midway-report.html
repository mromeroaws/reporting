Page purpose

Launch the new edition of the Structural Welding Code–Aluminum and send code users to order it.

Launch support

Four HubSpot email sends (Sep 4, 15, 24 and Oct 2), plus organic search and direct traffic. Paid search and social were minimal, with no dedicated launch campaign. Order and Purchase buttons send visitors to the publications store.

Summary

Four questions
What happened

The page drew 1,131 sessions and 1,009 users in 41 days. Launch week brought 543 sessions, 48% of the total. Weekly traffic then stepped down to about 50 sessions.

Why

Four HubSpot sends produced 46% of sessions and every traffic peak. Organic search added 29% at 93% engagement and kept arriving between sends. No paid campaign supported the launch.

Business impact

Visitors clicked the Order/Purchase buttons 428 times, but GA4 records $0 for the page. The code book checkout runs on a store GA4 can't see, so sales must come from that store.

What to improve

Get daily code book sales from the store. Build always-on search traffic. Use the next sends to cross-sell the CWI Aluminum Endorsement and the aluminum seminar.

Takeaways and next steps

Key takeaways

  1. Email launched the page.Four sends produced 46% of 1,131 sessions and every traffic peak. Between sends the page settles near 10 sessions a day.
  2. The audience is qualified.81.3% engagement overall, 97% for returning visitors and 93% from organic search. Visitors clicked the purchase buttons 428 times.
  3. Revenue is invisible, not absent.The code book sells outside GA4, so the page shows $0 even while it sends buyers to the store. The one tracked order was a $200 Affiliate membership.

Prioritized recommendations

  1. Close the revenue gap Priority 1Get daily D1.2:2026 unit sales from the publications store and line them up with the four send dates and the 428 button clicks. Then add cross-domain tracking or UTM-tagged buy links so orders flow back into GA4.
  2. Build the always-on baseline Priority 2Optimize the page for "D1.2 2026" and "aluminum structural welding code" searches, add FAQ structured data, and test a small branded paid-search campaign during the next send. Fix the no-slash URL variant.
  3. Cross-sell in the next sends Priority 3Target returning and engaged visitors with the CWI Aluminum Endorsement (no sales since launch) and the aluminum seminar. Send a reminder to recipients who didn't click.
