Page purpose

Turn companies looking for custom welding training into proposal requests through the HubSpot form “Corporate-Training-Solutions - Custom Seminars”.

What changed between periods

The page moved from /businesses/corporate-training-solutions/ on Mar 10, 2026 and launched with a new form (the old page used “Custom Seminar Inquiry”). Paid social ran continuously Jul–Dec 2025, stopped Jan 1, and returned as one burst Jul 29 – Aug 28, 2026.

Summary

Four questions
What happened

Sessions fell 37% to 5,081, but HubSpot form submissions rose from 7 to 11. The lead rate went from 0.09% to 0.22% of sessions.

Why

Paid social was 93–95% of landing traffic in both periods and is the whole traffic drop. Leads came from organic search, AI assistants, direct and email-matched contacts.

Business impact

About 1.4 proposal requests a month, 7 of 11 from business domains including Komatsu, Tesla and Smith+Nephew. None has a HubSpot deal yet, so revenue can't be measured.

What to improve

Retarget or pause paid social, grow search and AI visibility for code-specific training, and route every submission into a deal so the next report shows pipeline.

Takeaways and next steps

Key takeaways

  1. More leads from less traffic.11 HubSpot submissions vs. 7 on 37% fewer sessions. The lead rate rose 2.5× to 0.22%, and 8 of 11 came in months without paid social.
  2. Intent channels convert; reach doesn't.Organic search and AI assistants produced 6 of 11 leads. Paid social's 12,019 sessions over both periods produced 2 personal-email leads.
  3. Tracking now agrees.GA4 and HubSpot match exactly this period. The new form and the Aug–Sep 2025 GA4 misfire explain last period's differences.

Prioritized recommendations

  1. Retarget or pause paid social Priority 1Optimize any future corporate campaign for the form key event, target B2B job titles (QA, welding and plant managers), or move the budget to LinkedIn and paid search on “corporate welding training”.
  2. Grow search and AI visibility Priority 2Add FAQ and program content for code-specific seminars (D1.1, API 1104, ASME IX) and restore a recurring email to corporate contacts, the channel that fed most of last period's leads.
  3. Connect leads to revenue Priority 3Create a deal or sales-qualified stage for every submission so the next report can show pipeline and revenue, and prompt for a business email on the form.
