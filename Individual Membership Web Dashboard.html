Summary

Four questions
What happened

Sessions rose 53% to 15,254 and beat the traffic target. Engagement rate fell to 84.0% and time per user dropped to 0:44, both below plan. Membership purchases rose 50%, while membership revenue fell 5%.

Why

Paid campaigns sent 3,077 sessions to the Member page (Google Display 2,260, Paid Social 817). Those visits bounced more and produced 7 purchases. Display produced none. Student sign-ups nearly quadrupled.

Business impact

Conversion from Individual, Member and Early Career landings fell from 8.2% to 2.0%, under the 3.5% target. Without paid Display and Social it is 4.3%. Revenue is down $17K because renewals fell $22K.

What to improve

Redirect or rebuild the paid landing experience for the Member page. Get approval to launch the benefits redesign and geo-personalization. Push multi-year renewals.

  • Create behavior-based personalizations that promote Individual Membership content on Certification and Education pages as related content.
  • Launch geo-localized personalizations in states with lower membership numbers to increase acquisition there.
  • Redesign the page to present membership benefits more clearly and persuasively.

Takeaways and next steps

Key takeaways

  1. Traffic beat the target, but paid media drove most of the growth.15,254 sessions against a 10,122 target. Display and Paid Social sent 3,077 sessions to the Member page and produced 7 purchases.
  2. The Member page is pulling engagement below plan.The other three pages together hold a 91.8% engagement rate, in line with the 91.67% baseline. The Member page's bounce rate went from 5.6% to 24.3%.
  3. Student growth is strong, but renewals are slipping.New Student purchases rose from 615 to 2,332. Renewal revenue fell 10% ($232K to $210K), led by 3-year renewals.

Prioritized recommendations

  1. Fix the paid landing experience Priority 1Review Google Display placements, which sent 2,260 sessions with no purchases. Point paid traffic to /membership/individuals/ or a dedicated join page with plan options and a Join CTA above the fold.
  2. Unblock Tactics 3 and 4 Priority 2Get approval for the benefits redesign and geo-personalization, and finish the menu update. These address the time-on-page and engagement gaps. Retest the related-content block, which has a 0.34% CTR, with stronger offer copy and a higher placement.
  3. Protect renewal revenue Priority 3Promote 3-year renewals in renewal emails and checkout. Turn the Student surge into a Student → Early Career → Individual upgrade path.
